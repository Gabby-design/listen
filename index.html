Speech Engine Architecture

Listen provides dual engines for maximum speed, privacy, and zero latency.

100% Offline Built-In Zero Internet Needed
Windows Speech Platform

Local offlinetranscriber.exe uses Windows DictationGrammar. 100% of audio stays strictly on your computer. Zero latency, zero cloud uploads.

Cloud Fallback Sub-Second Whisper
Groq Cloud Whisper API

When online and configured, leverages Groq's LPUs running Whisper Large V3 Turbo for instant transcription of singing, humming, and technical vocabulary.

Intelligent GSMTC Media Management

Multi-Tab Aware Active
Title-Aware Pause & Resume

Queries Windows GSMTC sessions by title. Pauses only the specific playing tab (e.g. YouTube video), and resumes strictly that tab. Previously paused tabs (e.g. YouTube Music) remain untouched.

Core Advantages

Why Listen Solves What Others Broke

Traditional dictation apps force you to open bloated windows, copy-paste manually, or send all private audio to cloud servers. Listen fixes the foundation.

150 WPM vs 40 WPM

Human thought and speech effortlessly produce 150 words per minute. Average keyboard typing hovers at 40 WPM. Listen removes the mechanical tax on your cognition so your ideas flow directly to the screen.

Zero Focus Stealing

The borderless fluid orb hovers at your screen edge without stealing focus from your active window. Type in VS Code, Slack, Notion, Word, or Chrome without ever clicking away from your cursor.

100% Offline Dictation

Operates completely offline using native Windows Speech Platform APIs. Your voice never leaves your hardware unless you explicitly configure cloud Whisper mode. Complete privacy by default.

Smart Media Pausing

Targeted GSMTC media session inspection pauses only active playing audio (YouTube, Spotify) and resumes strictly that tab. Previously paused background tabs are never accidentally triggered.

Figures & Symbols Healing

Automatically formats figures and words ("2 + 2 = 4", "twenty percent" to "20%"), converts spoken punctuation, strips accidental hotkey taps under 400ms, and filters repetitive silence hallucinations.

Zero-Distraction Updater

Silent in-app background auto-updater via GitHub Releases keeps you on the latest version. Receive an unobtrusive notification and restart whenever you are ready.
